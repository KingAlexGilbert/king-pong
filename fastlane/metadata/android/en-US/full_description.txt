King Pong is a retro arcade game inspired by classic Pong, featuring an 18-level campaign, cross-platform online multiplayer, Royal Powerups, smarter CPU opponents, and a secret ending.

Features:
* 18 campaign levels across three leagues
* Worldwide online multiplayer with public matchmaking and private rooms
* Cross-platform matches between Android, Windows, Linux, and browser players
* Three Royal Powerups: Royal Split, Crown Rush, and Royal Guard
* Smarter CPU opponents with adjustable intelligence
* Custom games and local two-player mode
* LAN multiplayer with automatic discovery and QR pairing
* Level Select to replay completed campaign levels
* Progressive paddle shapes and changing arenas
* Touch, keyboard, and controller support
* Multiple languages and three local save slots
* Arcade sound effects and music
* Secret boss battle and ending

King Pong can be played offline and does not require an account. Online multiplayer is optional.

King Pong contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.
