=======
King Pong is a retro arcade Pong game featuring an 18-level campaign, cross-platform online multiplayer, Royal Powerups, smarter CPU opponents, local and LAN play, and a secret ending.

Features:
* 18 campaign levels across three leagues, plus Level Select for completed levels
* Worldwide online matchmaking and private rooms with optional host approval
* Cross-platform online play with browser, Android, Windows, and Linux players
* Rematches and customizable online arenas and paddles
* Three Royal Powerups: Royal Split, Crown Rush, and Royal Guard
* Smarter CPU opponents with adjustable intelligence in Custom Games
* Local two-player and LAN play, with QR pairing and native room discovery
* Progressive paddle shapes, changing arenas, and a secret boss battle
* Touch, keyboard, and controller controls
* Multiple languages and local save slots
* Arcade music, sound effects, and a secret ending soundtrack by Madelee

Single-player, local, and supported LAN modes can be played offline. Online multiplayer is optional and uses external networking services. No account is required. King Pong contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.
